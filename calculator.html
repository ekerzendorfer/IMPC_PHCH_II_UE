Scientific calculator

Evaluate your own expression. The calculator does not choose an equation or convert your units.

Multiply with * or ×. Enter 2.5e-12 for 2.5 × 10−12. A decimal comma also works.

—

Memory · M1–M3

Calculate first, then Store. Recall inserts the saved value. Slots are kept in this browser when storage is available.

Notation and constant reference

Use explicit multiplication, for example 2*pi. In DEG mode sin(30) returns 0.5 and inverse trig returns degrees. RAD uses radians. exp(x) means Euler’s number raised to x. EE enters the e in scientific notation. The key for elementary charge inserts q_e.

Values follow NIST / CODATA 2022. Use the stated units when combining values. Units are labels only and are not tracked by the calculator.
