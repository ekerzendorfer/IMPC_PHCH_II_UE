Mandatory Exam Basics

🧠 The 5 "By Heart" Formulas

These five fundamental equations are not printed on the exam formula sheet and must be known by heart:

$$\rho = \frac{m}{V}$$
$$n = \frac{m}{M}$$
$$c = \frac{n}{V}$$
$$p V = n R T$$
$$x_i = \frac{n_i}{n_{\text{tot}}}$$
